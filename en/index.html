The mission

An industrial bridge between Europe and Latin America

Applications are open for the Latin America Mission, the business mission promoted by Studio Armando Rossi & Partners with the participation of Italink and the institutional support of the Government of the State of Ceará, FIEC – the Federation of Industries of the State of Ceará, the Italian-Brazilian Chamber of Commerce and Industry and Sistema Italia.

The aim is to open real opportunities for internationalisation, export and industrial partnerships. This is not a ceremonial delegation: it is a working programme, with B2B meetings built around each participating company's profile, technical visits to the region's strategic infrastructure and direct roundtables with the institutions that decide on it.

Particular attention is devoted to the supply chains of design, furniture, natural stone, surfaces, materials, interiors and architecture, with the presentation of the Rinascimento Design District: the new ecosystem connecting industry, raw materials, innovation and logistics in a single integrated hub.

Why Ceará

A state that holds the entire value chain together

Raw materials, port logistics, renewable energy, technology and manufacturing in a single territory. That is what makes Ceará a gateway platform to the Latin American markets, rather than simply a destination.

01

Logistics and the Port of Pecém

A world-class logistics and port hub, with the FTZ Ceará and direct connections to Europe, the United States and Africa.

02

Energy and transition

A reference hub for green hydrogen, wind and solar: competitive renewable energy, a declared priority of the state.

03

Raw materials and manufacturing

Natural stone, materials and integrated advanced manufacturing: the basis of already structured, competitive supply chains.

04

Technology and training

Research, technological development and vocational training, with IEL and SENAI Ceará supporting new industrial settlements.

European presence

An ecosystem already connected to Europe

Ceará is already strongly connected to Europe through the presence of major European operators in its strategic infrastructure. The Port of Pecém relies on the strategic partnership of the Port of Rotterdam Authority, while Fortaleza International Airport is managed by the German group Fraport. In the energy sector, Enel is a well-established Italian presence in the territory.

The presence of major European operators in the state's strategic infrastructure strengthens Ceará as a natural platform for European companies looking to Brazil and Latin America.

Why take part

What a participating company takes home

A mission is only worth it if it produces relationships you can actually use once you are back. The programme is built on that principle.

1

A tailor-made B2B agenda

Meetings are defined from your company profile, in coordination with FIEC's industrial unions: you talk to relevant counterparts, not to a generic audience.

2

Direct access to institutions

The State Government, FIEC, the Italian-Brazilian Chamber and Sistema Italia take part in the mission: decision-makers, in the same room.

3

Technical visits

The Port of Pecém, the FTZ Ceará and industrial infrastructure are visited to assess the cost, timing and feasibility of a settlement or supply agreement.

4

A platform already mapped out

The Rinascimento Design District offers a structured entry route for companies evaluating production, showrooms or distribution in Brazil.

Why now

A complementary platform, not a relocation

For some energy-intensive industrial sectors, Europe has become more complex and more expensive. Rinascimento offers Italian companies a complementary platform for productive expansion in Brazil, with competitive renewable energy, ready industrial infrastructure and access to the markets of the Americas.

The logic is not “offshoring from Italy”, but:

  • 01Italian design, technology and know-how.
  • 02Competitive and sustainable production in Brazil.
  • 03Direct access to Latin American and international markets.

A particularly strong commercial case for ceramics, surfaces, glass, natural stone and every energy-intensive sector.

Subsidised finance for internationalisation

Support to finance your participation

We assist participating companies in identifying subsidised-finance instruments for internationalisation — in particular the subsidised-rate loans of Fund 394/81 managed by SIMEST, which can help cover the costs of taking part in international missions, trade fairs and events, with a possible non-repayable grant portion. Access to the incentives and their amount are assessed case by case by SIMEST, according to each company's requirements and the conditions in force.

What it can cover

The costs related to participation in international missions, trade fairs and events (promotional activities, organisation of the B2B meetings, consultancy and digital expenses) and, in the future, establishment investments in foreign markets.

Who it is for

Companies that meet the requirements set out by the SIMEST instruments.

Our role

We support you, together with our subsidised-finance consultant, in the feasibility analysis and in preparing the application.

This information is illustrative and does not constitute a guarantee that the incentives will be obtained, which remains subject to SIMEST's assessment and to the legislation in force at the time.

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before applying

Who can take part in the Latin America Mission?
The mission is open to Italian and European companies, professional firms and economic operators interested in export, industrial partnerships or productive settlement in Brazil. There is no minimum company size: what counts is sector relevance and a concrete objective.
What are the dates and duration?
The mission runs from 16 to 20 November 2026 in the State of Ceará, Brazil, based in Fortaleza. The programme covers five days of B2B meetings, technical visits and institutional appointments.
How much does participation cost?
Participation costs, including the organisational services provided, are communicated together with the detailed programme following your expression of interest. Travel and accommodation are indicated separately in the proposal.
Is the mission reserved for design and furniture?
No. Design, furniture and materials have a dedicated track through the Rinascimento Design District, but the mission is multi-sector and includes energy, logistics and infrastructure, machinery and plant engineering, digital technologies, agri-food and advanced manufacturing.
What kind of B2B meetings are planned?
Meetings with companies, FIEC industrial unions, local partners and institutions, selected on each participating company's profile. That is why the agenda is built in the weeks before departure: applying early improves the quality of the matches.
What role do the Brazilian institutions play?
The mission takes place with the institutional support of the Government of the State of Ceará, FIEC, the Italian-Brazilian Chamber of Commerce and Industry and Sistema Italia, ahead of the Cooperation Agreement with the Government of Ceará.
What is the Rinascimento Design District?
It is an integrated industrial ecosystem dedicated to design, materials, interiors and advanced manufacturing: a productive district connecting companies, suppliers, showrooms, logistics, services and innovation. It is presented during the mission and is open to collaboration, internationalisation and settlement projects.
How do I apply and by when?
