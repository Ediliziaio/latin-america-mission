Cookies and statistics

This page describes the measurement tools used by the Latin America Mission website.

Browser preferences

Your choice about optional cookies is saved in your browser. It is used to remember whether you accepted or refused statistics.

Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager

When measurement is enabled, Google Analytics is loaded through Google Tag Manager only after you consent to statistics. You can continue browsing if you refuse.

Information measured

Measurement concerns visits to public pages and use of the website. This integration does not send participation form fields to analytics tools. Search parameters and URL fragments are not included in the configured page information.

Changing your choice

Use the “Cookie preferences” button on the website to change your consent. Withdrawal stops measurement, removes analytics cookies accessible to the website and may reload the page.

Marketing

This integration does not add advertising pixels or remarketing tools.
